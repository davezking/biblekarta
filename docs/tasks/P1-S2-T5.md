## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** statistical sampling plus a rule that feeds back across rounds.
- **Review:** `auto`. It's deterministic and fully tested.

## Context
PRD v5.4:
- **Spot-check 2–5%** of the *unflagged* verses at random against the scan.
- **If the error rate exceeds 0.5%, widen the check book by book.**

The flags are in `work/flags.json`; the anchor and chronology verses are verified separately (their own sheet). Decisions from earlier rounds are in `decisions/round-N.jsonl` (written by P1-S3-T1; it may not exist yet).

## Do this
1. **`sample_core.draw(verses, flagged, rate, seed, min_per_book=5)`:** a stratified random sample per book, proportional to the book's verse count, with at least `min_per_book`.
   - Exclude flagged verses and anchor-sheet verses.
   - It's reproducible: the seed is stored.
   - The default rate is 3%.
2. **`sample_core.escalate(decisions_for_round, sample)`:**
   - For each book, the error rate = sampled verses whose decision was `Fix` ÷ sampled verses reviewed.
   - A book above **0.5%** gets an escalation for the next round: a fresh sample at 20% of its unflagged verses (configurable), excluding verses already checked.
   - A book that's still above 0.5% after a 20% sample escalates to **100%** of its verses.
3. **`bk sample draw --round 1 [--rate 0.03] [--seed N]`** writes `rounds/samples/round-1.json` (`{seed, rate, refs: [...]}`). **`bk sample escalate --round 1`** reads the round-1 decisions and writes `rounds/samples/round-2.json` with the escalations, printing a per-book table (sampled, errors, rate, action).

## Acceptance criteria
- The same seed gives the same sample.
- The sample size is between 2% and 5% of the unflagged verses at the default rate, with at least 5 per book.
- A book with 1 error in 100 sampled verses (1%) is escalated; 0 in 100 is not.
- The 20% → 100% escalation path works.

## Tests to write or update
- `test_sample.py`: determinism, the rate bounds, stratification, both escalation steps, and the exclusion of flagged and anchor verses.

## How to test manually
1. Run `uv run --project tools bk sample draw --round 1` → it prints about 900 verses (3% of the unflagged) and the counts per book.
2. Open `rounds/samples/round-1.json` → the seed and refs are listed.
