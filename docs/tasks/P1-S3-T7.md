## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** a precise text rule plus a strict display component.
- **Review:** `auto`. Golden tests pin the rule, and the reviewer approves it visually (R5).

## Context
- Stored verse text has **single spaces between words and no ፡** (`/data/scripture/<id>.json`, from P1-S3-T5).
- **PRD v5.4:** ፡ is generated at display time by a fixed rule: one ፡ between words, and none next to ። ፣ ፤ ፥ or ?. The reviewer approves the rule against sample scan pages.
- FR-17: verse text is otherwise shown exactly; only styling may vary.
- The PRD's out-of-scope list excludes a public continuous reader, so the viewer is **preview-only** under `/dev/`.
- Book names and order come from `books.ts`.

## Do this
1. **`src/lib/core/separators.ts`:** `wordSeparators(t: string): string`.
   - Split on single spaces.
   - Between two adjacent tokens, insert ፡ **only if** the left token doesn't end with one of ። ፣ ፤ ፥ ? and the right token doesn't start with one of them.
   - Otherwise keep the single space, as printed.
   - Export `SEPARATOR_RULE_VERSION = 1` and `SEPARATOR_RULE_STATUS = 'pending-review'` (an i18n-labeled badge on previews until the reviewer approves; then change it to `'approved'` in a PR).
   - Never alter any other character.
2. **`refs.ts`:** `formatRef(ref, books, {short})` joins chapter and verse with ፥ and ranges with –. The short form uses `abbr_am` when present, otherwise the scan `name_am`. Also export `parseRefKey('1ki.16.29')`.
3. **`VerseText.tsx`:** props `{verses: {n, t, footnote?}[]}`. It renders `<p lang="am" class="ms">` with a red Ge'ez verse number, then **`wordSeparators(t)` as a plain text node** (no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, no other transform). Footnotes aren't rendered (R6 is undecided); leave a `TODO(R6)` comment.
4. **`/dev/scripture/`** lists the verified books in scan order, with a "pending ፡ rule" badge if it isn't approved. **`/dev/scripture/[book]`** shows the chapters with red `ምዕራፍ <geez>` headings and the verses.
5. **`/dev/scripture/separator-samples`** shows about 30 real verses chosen automatically to cover the edge cases: verses with ። ፣ ፤ ፥ ? mid-verse; a verse ending in ?; a single-word verse; a verse with an allowlisted digit. Each shows its reference, the stored text in a monospace box and the rendered text side by side, so the reviewer can compare it with the scan pages.

## Acceptance criteria
- Golden: `"እግዚአብሔርም አለ ብርሃን ይሁን፤ ብርሃንም ሆነ።"` → `"እግዚአብሔርም፡አለ፡ብርሃን፡ይሁን፤ ብርሃንም፡ሆነ።"`. No ፡ is ever placed next to ። ፣ ፤ ፥ ?.
- For a rendered verse, DOM `textContent === wordSeparators(stored t)` exactly.
- Removing all ፡ from the output and replacing them with spaces gives back the stored text exactly (the rule is lossless).
- `/dev/*` is absent from a production build.

## Tests to write or update
- `separators.test.ts`: the golden cases (≥ 10, including each punctuation mark on each side, `?`, and single words), the lossless property over all verses of the fixture book, and idempotence on text without ፡.
- `VerseText.test.tsx`: DOM equality.
- `refs.test.ts`: the short and long forms, ranges, and parsing.

## How to test manually
1. Open `<preview>/dev/scripture/1ki/` on your phone → the verses have ፡ between words, and no ፡ next to ፤ or ።.
2. Open `<preview>/dev/scripture/separator-samples` next to the scan pages for the same verses → the separators match the print. Note any mismatch for the reviewer.
