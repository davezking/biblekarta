## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** several layers built on patterns that `ReignLayer`/`LabelLayer` (P2-S2-T6) already set.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- FR-5: the prophets are in a ribbon above the kingdom streams, and approximate spans are drawn with a **dashed outline**. In the tile, their spans come packed into rows with `dashed` flags (P2-S2-T2).
- Board 5 (`design-reference/board5-desktop.html`) also shows:
  - events in their stream: ፨ ቤተ መቅደስ ተሠራ (በግምት 966), the 931 split, Shishak's invasion in 925;
  - people in the neighbour lanes: Ethbaal in Sidon; Ben-Hadad and Hazael in Aram; Shalmaneser III in Assyria;
  - the Queen of Sheba in the **Ethiopia** lane.

  Match its marks and lettering.
- DR-10: figures appear inside streams for major rulers and prophets; they are `aria-hidden`. Use `Figure` from the sprite (P2-S1-T3). The sprite is already mounted once by `Chart.tsx`.
- The same rules as the other layers apply: geometry in the scaled group, text and figures in screen space, LOD via `lod.ts`, no commits during gestures, touch hit targets ≥ 44 px. `ProphetLayer`, `EventLayer` and `FigureLayer` are stubs from P2-S2-T4.

## Do this
1. **`ProphetLayer`:** the prophet spans in the ribbon rows. A `dashed: true` span gets a dashed outline. Names are lettered with LOD, and approximate spans carry the "በግምት" form from `formatScholarly`.
2. **`EventLayer`:**
   - A point event is a ፨ mark plus its title. A span event is a thin bar.
   - Each sits in its `stream_id`'s lane, or in the top lane band if it has none.
   - Titles follow LOD, by `importance`; events without a visible title still have a tooltip.
3. **People in lanes:** people whose stream is a `lane` (neighbours, Ethiopia) are drawn as their reign segment if they have a reign, otherwise as a marker at their placed date, with the name lettered as in Board 5.
4. **`FigureLayer`:** a figure for each person with `importance ≥ 4`, and for every prophet, placed inside their segment when it is wide enough on screen (figure size + padding), and hidden otherwise. The size follows the lane height (24–40 px).
5. Every item is selectable (sets `selection` to `p:<slug>` or `e:<slug>`), with a long-press or hover tooltip.

## Acceptance criteria
- Elisha's span (approximate, 848–797) has a dashed outline, and overlapping prophets never overlap visually.
- Board 5's events and neighbour people appear in the right lanes, and the Queen of Sheba is in the Ethiopia lane.
- Figures appear only when they fit, are `aria-hidden`, and use the sprite (`<use>`, not inline paths).
- No React commits during a simulated gesture.
