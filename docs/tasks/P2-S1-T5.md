## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** a page that lays out existing components.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- Sprint 1 built the tokens (`tokens.ts`, `contrast.ts`), `HaragBand`/`TitleCartouche`, `Figure`, the UI primitives and `Panel`.
- `/dev/*` is preview-only. The `devOnly()` integration strips it from production builds.
- The church reviewer will look at this page to sign off the visual language before the chart is built on it (R13).

## Do this
1. **`/dev/design`:** one page with these sections:
   - **Inks & pigments:** a swatch for every token, with its hex value. For each `TEXT_ON_FILL` pair, a text sample with its contrast ratio (pass/fail).
   - **Typography:** Abyssinica titles in red ink, body in black, and UI text in Noto Sans Ethiopic. Include the no-bold rule and a ፡ example.
   - **Harag:** every height, plus the title cartouche.
   - **Figures:** king, queen and prophet × every tone × sizes 24 / 48 / 120.
   - **UI:** every primitive in its states (default, hover, focus, disabled), and a button that opens the `Panel`.
2. **Link it from `/dev/`.**

## Acceptance criteria
- Every primitive and component from Sprint 1 appears at least once.
- The page has no console errors, and works at 390 px and 1440 px wide.
- `/dev/design` is absent from a production build (the existing prod-excludes-dev check covers `/dev/`).

## Tests to write or update
