## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** a small, local placement fix with a test.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- This is a follow-up bug, found while checking P2-S3-T7 on the seeded build.
- LabelLayer hides a reign label whose box overlaps its stream's name. The stream names are lettered at the left of the visible part of each stream.
- EventLayer's `ItemsOverlay` letters event and person titles without that check. So near a stream's left edge, a title can be drawn over the stream name.
- Repro: on the seeded preview, zoom in around 925 BC with the Shishak invasion (Egypt lane) near the left edge. Its title "ሺሻቅ ይሁዳን ወረረ (925 ዓ.ዓ.)" sits on "ግብፅ ፡ 22ኛው ሥርወ መንግሥት".

## Do this
1. **`LabelLayer.tsx`:** move the stream-name placement into a pure, exported `streamNameBoxes(names, viewport, measure)`, and export `streamNames`, `Box` and `overlaps`. `placeNames` uses the helper, so both layers compute the same name boxes.
2. **`EventLayer.tsx` (`ItemsOverlay`):**
   - It takes the stream names and computes their boxes each frame (cheap: one per stream).
   - It hides a title whose box overlaps a name. That item is then not "shown", so it gets the hover / long-press tooltip.
   - On pan frames (`panShifting`), items near the names' zone at the left of the screen are re-checked, along with the edge items.
3. **`ProphetLayer.tsx`:** pass the stream names to `ItemsOverlay`, as EventLayer does.

## Acceptance criteria
- No event or person title is drawn over a stream name, at any zoom or pan.
- A title hidden this way still has its tooltip, and comes back once its item moves clear of the name.
- No React commits during gestures. The existing chart tests pass.

## Tests to write or update
- `EventLayer.test.tsx`:
  - The Shishak title is hidden at the left edge, by the Egypt name. It has a tooltip on hover, and is lettered again once panned clear.
  - `titleBox` measures from the baseline and the font size.

## How to test manually
1. On the seeded preview, zoom in near 925 BC and pan so the Shishak invasion's ፨ mark sits at the left edge. Its title hides rather than covering "ግብፅ ፡ 22ኛው ሥርወ መንግሥት"; hover the mark to see it in the tooltip.
2. Pan right: the title comes back once it clears the name.
