## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** every later layer depends on this mapping, and it decides how two different chronologies share one chart.
- **Review:** `opus`. A mistake here misplaces every item on the chart.

## Context
- `CLAUDE.md`: **only `timescale.ts` maps a year to x.** Traditional years are AA integers (creation = AA 1). Scholarly years are astronomical (1 BC = 0). Convert only via `src/lib/core/dates.ts` (`astroToBcAd`, `aaToAmeteMihret`, `ameteMihretToGregorianRange`, `spanOf`).
- Architecture §3: the traditional axis is linear in AA years. The scholarly axis is linear in astronomical years, **plus a tradition-only band** for pre-Abraham items that have only a traditional date (R10). Phase 2's data doesn't use the band yet, but it must exist and be tested.
- `DateValue` (in `schema.ts`): `method: "relative"` has no year, so it can't be placed on an axis. Demo step 7 says such items are **counted, not shown**.
- FR-6: zoom from the whole-Bible view down to single years. The chart zooms horizontally only.
- The compiler (P2-S2-T3) puts the config in `/data/index.json`. The chart (P2-S2-T4) and the axes (P2-S2-T5) call this module.

## Do this
1. **Types:**
   - `Axis = 'scholarly' | 'traditional'`.
   - `TimescaleConfig = {scholarly: {min, max}; traditional: {min, max}; pxPerYear; band: {aaMin, aaMax, widthPx, gapPx} | null; zoom: {kMin, kMax}}`. `kMax` is set so that one year is at least ~40 px.
   - `buildTimescaleConfig(eras)` derives the domains from the live eras' `YearPair`s, plus a margin.
2. **`x(year, axis, cfg)` and `invert(px, axis, cfg)`:** linear, at zoom k = 1. The chart's d3-zoom transform applies k.
3. **The tradition-only band:** on the scholarly axis it sits left of the scholarly domain, separated by `gapPx`. Inside it, items are placed linearly by their AA year.
   - `xBand(aa, cfg)`; `invert` reports `{band: true, aa}` for points in the band.
4. **`place(item, axis, cfg)`:** takes the item's `{scholarly?: DateValue; traditional?: DateValue}` and returns `{kind: 'axis' | 'band', x0, x1}` or `{kind: 'unplaced', reason}`.
   - Use `spanOf()` for the years.
   - A `relative` date, or no date on that axis, gives `unplaced`.
   - On the scholarly axis, an item with no scholarly date and a traditional `certainty: "tradition_only"` date goes in the band.
   - A point date gets a 1-year width.
5. **`ticks(axis, k, [px0, px1], cfg)`:** returns tick years with a `level` (`century` | `half` | `decade` | `year`), choosing the finest level whose labels stay ≥ 48 px apart. Half-centuries are flagged for red ink (DR-7).
6. **`equivalentYear(year, from, to)`:** for keeping the view centred when the axis switches. It's a **calendar** conversion done only through `dates.ts`, not a chronology claim. Say so in its doc comment.

## Acceptance criteria
- `x` and `invert` round-trip exactly on whole years, on both axes.
- Order is preserved: the earlier year is always to the left, including band vs axis (the band is left of the earliest scholarly year).
- `relative` and missing dates are `unplaced`, never given an invented position.
- At the whole-domain zoom (`kMin`), the live domain fits a 1440 px viewport; at `kMax`, one year is ≥ 40 px.
- No other module computes x from a year (the reviewer checks with grep in later PRs).

## Tests to write or update
- `timescale.test.ts`: round trips; ordering; band placement and gap; `place()` for every `method` and `certainty`; tick level choice across k (century → decade → year); half-century flags; `equivalentYear` vs `dates.ts` examples; and a property test over random years.

## How to test manually
1. Run `pnpm test -- timescale` → all pass.
2. In a REPL (`pnpm tsx`), `place(ahab, 'traditional', cfg)` for the seed Ahab reign → `unplaced` (relative TBD), and `place(ahab, 'scholarly', cfg)` → the 874–853 BC span.
