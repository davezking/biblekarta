## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** the grouping rules (፻ and ፼) are subtle, and both directions are needed.
- **Review:** `auto`. It's pure and exhaustively tested.

## Context
Ge'ez numerals are used on the top chart axis (e.g. ፲፻ = 1000, ፱፻፶ = 950), for verse numbers and for chapter headings (ምዕራፍ ፭). The digits are ፩–፱ and ፲–፺, plus ፻ (100) and ፼ (10,000).

## Do this
In `src/lib/core/geez.ts`, export:
- `toGeez(n)` for 1 ≤ n ≤ 99,999,999
- `fromGeez(s)`, which throws `GeezParseError` on invalid input

Follow the standard rules: there's no zero; 100 is ፻ (not ፩፻); 10,000 is ፼; pairs of digits join with ፻ and ፼ alternately (12345 = ፼፳፫፻፵፭).

## Acceptance criteria
- The golden table passes.
- `fromGeez(toGeez(n)) === n` for 1..20,000.
- Invalid input (empty, `፻፻`, mixed digits) throws.

## Tests to write or update
- `geez.test.ts`: 1 ፩, 10 ፲, 11 ፲፩, 100 ፻, 101 ፻፩, 200 ፪፻, 950 ፱፻፶, 1000 ፲፻, 1990 ፲፱፻፺, 10000 ፼, 12345 ፼፳፫፻፵፭; the round-trip property; the invalid cases.
