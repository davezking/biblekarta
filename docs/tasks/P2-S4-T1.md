## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** presenting dates honestly (method, certainty, source) across every `DateValue` combination.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- FR-21: every detail panel shows **both dates** and a certainty label (exact, approximate, range, disputed, tradition-only).
- FR-22: each date records its method (anchor, derived, traditional, relative). FR-23: the panel explains **in plain Amharic how each date is known**. FR-24: dates derived from a corrected anchor are flagged (`isStale()` in `dates.ts`).
- Demo step 5: tap Ahab → he's outlined in red, and a side panel (a bottom sheet on a phone) opens with:
  - his figure;
  - "874–853 ዓ.ዓ." with a certainty chip;
  - the traditional row ("TBD" via relative);
  - "እንዴት ታወቀ?", which explains the method.
- The tile has everything the panel needs: people, reigns, events, their `DateValue`s, refs, notes, and the `sources` and `anchors` they cite (P2-S2-T3).
- Use `formatScholarly` / `formatTraditional` (`dates.ts`), `Panel`, `Chip` and `Disclosure` (P2-S1-T4), and `Figure` (P2-S1-T3).
- `PanelSlot.tsx` is the stub P2-S2-T4 mounted in the chart, and selection comes from the store (P2-S3-T6).
- **This task owns all the Sprint 4 panel strings,** including the ones P2-S4-T2 renders, so that task doesn't touch the i18n files.

## Do this
1. **`PanelSlot`:** when `selection` is set, open `Panel` with `DetailPanel`. Closing it clears the selection, which updates the URL through the existing sync.
2. **`DetailPanel`:**
   - for a person: their figure, name (Amharic; English name small), their reign(s), a date block, their refs (a placeholder list that P2-S4-T2 replaces) and an editor note slot;
   - for an event: its title, summary, date block and refs.
3. **`DateRow`:** one row per axis: the label (the axis name), the formatted date, or for `relative` the `relative_text_am` (TBD shows as `date.tbd`), and a certainty `Chip`.
   - Both rows always show, even when one is TBD.
   - A stale date (`isStale`) gets a red-ink warning line.
4. **`HowKnown`:** a `Disclosure` "እንዴት ታወቀ?" that explains each date in a sentence per method:
   - **anchor:** the anchor's label and its external source (e.g. Kurkh Monolith);
   - **derived:** "calculated from <anchor label> (<anchor value used>)", plus the derivation refs;
   - **traditional:** the source citation;
   - **relative:** the relative text.

   Amharic templates go in `am.json` and English in `en.json`. Put a `_comment` at the top of `am.json` saying the wording is **pending the reviewer's approval** (R13).
5. **i18n for P2-S4-T2:**
   - `verse.not_ready` = "ይህ መጽሐፍ በዝግጅት ላይ ነው"
   - `verse.editor_note` (the label for team-written notes, FR-18)
   - `verse.credit` (FR-19): **placeholder text marked pending: the Society approves the exact wording.** Don't write the credit text as if it were final.
   - `verse.footnotes` = "ማስታወሻ" (used only if R6 is approved)

## Acceptance criteria
- Tapping Ahab opens the panel with "874–853 ዓ.ዓ.", the TBD traditional row, a certainty chip, and the How-known text naming the Qarqar anchor and the Kurkh Monolith.
- Both date rows appear for every person and event in the seed, and nothing shows a year for a `relative` date.
- It's a side panel at ≥ 1024 px and a bottom sheet below that. Closing it clears the selection and focus returns to the chart.
- Every user-facing string comes from i18n, and the figure is `aria-hidden`.
