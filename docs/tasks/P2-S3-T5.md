## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** a mapping between two coordinate systems, plus a draggable control.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- FR-7: a minimap of **all eras** shows and controls the visible window.
- Board 5 has the minimap strip at the bottom: "መላው ሰንጠረዥ — ከፍጥረት እስከ ሐዋርያት", with the eras ፍጥረት, አባቶች, ዘፀአት, መሳፍንት, የነገሥታት ዘመን, ምርኮ, በኪዳናት መካከል, ወንጌላት and ሐዋርያት. Match its look.
- Eras come from `index.json`, ordered by `order`. **Coming-soon eras have no dates** (the schema only requires dates for live eras), so the minimap can't be proportional to years for them. Use `minimap_weight`, defaulting to 1.
- Phase 2 demo, known limitations: only the Divided Kingdom is live, and the other eras show "coming soon" (that i18n key exists from P2-S2-T4).
- Move the chart only through `ChartController` (P2-S2-T4). Read the live viewport from the throttled viewport channel, imperatively: no React commits during gestures.

## Do this
1. **`minimap.ts` (pure):** `minimapLayout(eras, widthPx)` → a box per era (weights, in order).
   - `viewToMinimap(view, era layout, cfg)` and `minimapToView(px, …)` map between the chart's visible year range and the minimap. A live era's box maps linearly to its years.
2. **`Minimap.tsx`:** the era boxes, with their names in Abyssinica; live eras in ink, coming-soon eras faded with the "coming soon" label on hover or focus.
   - **The window rectangle** follows the chart viewport (imperative updates).
   - **Dragging the window** pans the chart.
   - **Tapping a live era** focuses its span (`focusOn`). Tapping a coming-soon era does nothing.
   - Keyboard: the window is a slider-like control (arrows pan).
   - Touch: hit areas ≥ 44 px tall.

## Acceptance criteria
- Dragging the window pans the chart, and panning the chart moves the window, in both directions and at any zoom.
- Every era from the index appears in `order`, and coming-soon eras are visibly distinct and not clickable.
- No React commits while the chart is being dragged, or while the window is being dragged.
- Works at 390 px wide without horizontal overflow.

## Tests to write or update
- `minimap.test.ts`: weights, the round trip view ↔ minimap, clamping at the ends.
- `Minimap.test.tsx`: dragging calls `setView`, the imperative window updates, keyboard panning, coming-soon behaviour.

## How to test manually
1. On the seeded preview, drag the minimap window → the chart pans. Pan the chart → the window follows.
2. Tap a faded era → nothing happens, and it's labelled "coming soon". (The seed gets its coming-soon eras in P2-SEED; until then, check this with the test fixture index.)
