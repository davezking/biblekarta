## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** rendering from `ticks()`, with a couple of placement rules.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- DR-7: a decade ruling grid across the whole chart. The **top** axis uses Ge'ez numerals (e.g. ፲፻, ፱፻፶) with half-centuries in red. The **bottom** axis uses Arabic numerals.
- Demo step 2: zooming in on 850 BC takes the ticks from decades to single years.
- `ticks(axis, k, range, cfg)` (P2-S2-T1) gives the tick years and their level. Use `toGeez` (`geez.ts`) and `astroToBcAd` (`dates.ts`) for the numbers. Don't compute x yourself: use `x()` from `timescale.ts`.
- The geometry `<g>` is scaled in x only, so **text must never be inside it** (it would stretch). Lines may be inside it, with a non-scaling stroke. Labels are screen-space and re-placed through the throttled viewport channel (see `zoom.ts` from P2-S2-T4), with **no React re-render during a gesture**.
- The traditional axis shows AA years; the axis label prefix `axis.traditional.prefix` is already in `am.json` (still pending the reviewer's approval, R11).
- `GridLayer` and `AxisLayer` exist as stubs from P2-S2-T4. Fill them in; don't edit `Chart.tsx`.

## Do this
1. **`GridLayer`:** decade lines (thin, low-contrast ink) inside the geometry group across the full chart height. Century lines are slightly stronger; half-century lines are red ink.
2. **`AxisLayer`:** two screen-space tick-label rows, top and bottom.
   - The top row is Ge'ez in Abyssinica; half-centuries are red.
   - The bottom row is Arabic in Abyssinica.
   - On the scholarly axis the labels show BC/AD years (`astroToBcAd`); on the traditional axis, AA years.
   - On gesture ticks, update the label nodes' `x` imperatively from the viewport channel. At gesture end, recompute the tick set (one React commit).
3. Labels never overlap: drop a label rather than overlap (the 48 px spacing from `ticks()` should already ensure this).

## Acceptance criteria
- At the whole-era zoom, the ticks are centuries or half-centuries; zoomed in on 850 BC, single years are labelled.
- The top labels are valid Ge'ez numerals for the BC year (`fromGeez(label) === year`), and half-centuries are red.
- No `<text>` element is inside the scaled geometry group.
- No React commits happen during a simulated gesture (the same Profiler pattern as P2-S2-T4).

## Tests to write or update
- `GridLayer.test.tsx`: the line counts per level, red half-centuries.
- `AxisLayer.test.tsx`: Ge'ez/Arabic labels for a known range, BC conversion, no text in the geometry group, imperative updates without commits.

## How to test manually
1. Open the seeded preview and zoom from the whole era to 850 BC → the labels move from centuries to decades to single years, with Ge'ez on top and Arabic on the bottom.
2. Check that 900 and 850 are red on the top axis.
