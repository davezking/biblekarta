## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** two straightforward layers, plus an accessible popover.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- FR-23 and DR-8: anchor dates are drawn as **red dashed vertical lines, full chart height**.
  - Demo step 4: tapping one shows its label and source, e.g. "Qarqar — Kurkh Monolith" and "Jehu's tribute — Black Obelisk".
  - Anchors are in `index.json` (`label_am`, `label_en`, `date.start`, `external_source`, `confidence`). Seed: `qarqar-853` (−852) and `jehu-tribute-841` (−840).
- Anchors are external evidence dated on the **scholarly** axis only. On the traditional axis they have no position; don't convert them.
- The **tradition-only band** (R10) is on the scholarly axis, left of the scholarly domain, when `cfg.band` is present (P2-S2-T1). Phase 2's data has nothing in it, but the band must render correctly when the config has one.
- `Era.gap: true` marks a period the chart shows as a gap. Its label is centred in the gap.
- The i18n keys (band label, gap label) were added by P2-S2-T4; this task adds none. `AnchorLayer` and `BandLayer` are stubs from P2-S2-T4.

## Do this
1. **`AnchorLayer`:** a full-height red dashed line per anchor on the scholarly axis (non-scaling stroke, in the scaled group). Make a ≥ 44 px-wide invisible hit area in screen space.
   - Tap, click, or Enter on focus opens a small popover: the label in the current UI language, "—", the external source, and the confidence.
   - Esc or tapping elsewhere closes it.
   - Use `ui/Tooltip` or a small popover built from the UI primitives.
2. **`BandLayer`:** when `cfg.band` exists and the axis is scholarly, a lightly tinted band with its label (red ink, Abyssinica) and a gap separating it from the scholarly axis.
3. **Gap labels:** for each era with `gap: true`, a centred label in its span.
4. **Traditional axis:** no anchor lines, and no band.

## Acceptance criteria
- The seed shows red dashed lines at 853 and 841 BC. Tapping each shows "ቃርቃር ጦርነት — Kurkh Monolith" and the Black Obelisk entry.
- The anchor lines are keyboard-focusable, with an accessible name.
- With a test config that has a band, the band renders left of the earliest scholarly year, with its label. Without one, nothing renders.
- No anchor lines or band on the traditional axis.
