## Why this model / review tier
- **Model:** a small, local layout fix with tests.
- **Review:** `auto`.

## Context
- Follow-up bug. `reignBoxes()` (`ReignLayer.tsx`) puts a reign into a packed row whenever `band.rows > 0 && reign.row >= 0`. A polity band can have rows too, because they hold its events and people. But its reigns are always fixed at row 0 and should fill the stream's full width. `layoutStreams` (`src/lib/layout/streams.ts`) says: "Reigns in a polity sit in row 0; everything else is packed".
- Repro: `BK_SEED=1 pnpm dev`. The United Kingdom has a temple event, so David's and Solomon's reigns get a 32 px slot. Their dividers are short, their labels and selection outline sit on the packed row, and FigureLayer (which sizes figures from `reignBoxes`) gives them a 24 px figure instead of 40 px.
- The reign branch of `selectionOutlines` (`OverlayLayer.tsx`) has the same test and the same bug.

## Do this
1. **`ReignLayer.tsx`:** add `inPackedRow(band, row)`. It is false for a polity band (`band.kind === 'polity'`); otherwise it is `band.rows > 0 && row >= 0`. `reignBoxes` uses it, so a polity's reigns get the full-height slot.
2. **`OverlayLayer.tsx`:** the reign outlines (a selected reign, and a selected person's reigns) use `inPackedRow`. Event and people outlines stay on their packed rows.

## Acceptance criteria
- In a polity with packed rows, a reign's fill, dividers and selection outline span the stream's full width (`band.cy ± band.height / 2`).
- Its label is centred on the stream, and its hit target is the stream's full width (at least 44 px).
- Reigns in a non-polity stream with rows (the prophets ribbon) still fill their packed row.
- Events and people in a polity still sit in their packed rows.

## Tests to write or update
- `ReignLayer.test.tsx`: in the seed, the United Kingdom has rows > 0, and David's and Solomon's dividers and fills span its full width. A selected Solomon is outlined over the full width.
- `LabelLayer.test.tsx`: Solomon's label is shown in full and centred on the United Kingdom stream, and his hit target is the stream's full width.
